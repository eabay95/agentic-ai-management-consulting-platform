Market Basket Recommendation


PAGE 7
As a Data Science student at San José State University, you have been approached by an electronic retailer to design 
and implement a Market Basket Recommendation System for their online checkout process. The primary objective is 
to enhance the customer’s shopping experience by intelligently recommending additional products that are frequently 
purchased together.
When a shopper visits the retailer’s website, the checkout cart begins empty. As soon as the customer adds the first 
item, the system should analyze historical transaction data to identify items that have been commonly purchased 
alongside that product. The model then generates a ranked list of recommendations, presenting them as helpful 
suggestions to the user.
Each time the customer adds a new item—either from the recommended list or by freely selecting a different 
product—the system dynamically updates its recommendations based on the current combination of items in the 
cart. This iterative recommendation process continues throughout the checkout experience, allowing the model to 
refine its predictions as new items are added.
 
Electronic Retailer Market Basket Recommendation System for Online Checkout Integration

In some instances, the combination of selected items may not exist in the historical transactions dataset. In such 
cases, the model should gracefully handle the absence of patterns and refrain from displaying irrelevant 
recommendations. This ensures that the system remains both accurate and contextually relevant.
As a Data Science major, your role involves designing, developing, and deploying this recommendation model. Once 
trained, the model can be deployed as a FAST API or microservice, integrated with the retailer’s checkout system to 
deliver real-time recommendations. The deployment phase would also involve model validation, performance 
monitoring, and periodic retraining to ensure continued relevance as shopping trends evolve.
